title: lemon Schema Terms

lemon Schema Terms

This MASP Schema was auto-generated from the lemon (Lexicon Model for Ontologies) RDF vocabulary, https://lemon-model.net/lemon.rdf, using the scripts/owl-to-masp.py script. lemon describes lexical information about ontology elements and other RDF resources: lexicons, lexical entries and their forms, senses, syntactic frames, phrase structure and morphology. It is the model that the W3C OntoLex-Lemon vocabulary was later based on; the terms here are in lemon's own namespace, http://lemon-model.net/lemon#.

Conversion notes:

  • Labels and comments are in English; lemon's labels in other languages are not carried over.
  • Anonymous owl:Restriction superclasses (e.g. cardinality constraints on LexicalEntry and Lexicon) are dropped, as for other converted ontologies.
