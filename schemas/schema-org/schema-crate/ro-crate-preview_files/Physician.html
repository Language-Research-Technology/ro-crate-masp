Physician

https://schema.org/Physician

An individual physician or a physician's office considered as a [[MedicalOrganization]].

Subclass of: MedicalBusiness, MedicalOrganization

Subclasses (2)

  • IndividualPhysician — An individual medical practitioner. For their official address use [[address]], for affiliations to hospitals use [[hospitalAffiliation]]. The [[practicesAt]] property can be used to indicate [[MedicalOrganization]] hospitals, clinics, pharmacies etc. where this physician practices.
  • PhysiciansOffice — A doctor's office or clinic.
