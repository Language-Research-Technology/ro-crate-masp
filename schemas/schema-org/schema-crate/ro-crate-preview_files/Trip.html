Trip

https://schema.org/Trip

A trip or journey. An itinerary of visits to one or more places.

Subclass of: Intangible

Subclasses (5)

  • BoatTrip — A trip on a commercial ferry line.
  • BusTrip — A trip on a commercial bus line.
  • Flight — An airline flight.
  • TouristTrip — A tourist trip. A created itinerary of visits to one or more places of interest ([[TouristAttraction]]/[[TouristDestination]]) often linked by a similar theme, geographic area, or interest to a particular [[touristType]]. The [UNWTO](http://www2.unwto.org/) defines tourism trip as the Trip taken by visitors. (See examples below.)
  • TrainTrip — A trip on a commercial train line.
